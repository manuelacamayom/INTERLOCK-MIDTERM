INTERLOCK

Interlock is an interactive collage-website that treats architectural representation as something you don’t just look at, but physically navigate and assemble. The home screen begins as a fragmented map, an image broken into pieces, so the project opens through the act of putting things together. Once the map resolves, hotspots act like “locks” into different rooms of the project: Pool, Exhibition Space, Drawings, Housing, Threshold, Context, Purpose, Intent, and Process. Each hotspot leads to a new page that behaves like a digital desk: images, drawings, plans, axons, models, and text become movable elements that the viewer can drag, layer, and rearrange, building their own sequence and meaning rather than following a fixed slideshow. The website is intentionally non-linear because the project itself is about relationships, how spaces connect, how parts meet, and how an idea is understood through adjacency. Meaning emerges through overlap, friction, and alignment. By turning documentation into an interactive field, INTERLOCK asks the viewer to participate in the construction of the project’s story, moving through it like a space, and reading it through the act of connection rather than through a single framed image.
